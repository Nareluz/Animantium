About Animantium

Animantium is a free educational website dedicated to Cambridge IGCSE Biology (0610). It was created to make biology easier to understand through clear explanations, concise study notes and visual learning resources. The website covers the topics included in the Cambridge IGCSE Biology syllabus and is open to all students who wish to learn, revise and explore biology.
