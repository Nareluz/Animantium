About Animantium

Why does Animantium exist?

Once, I was an IGCSE student, and finding study materials that actually helped me was difficult. That is why I am now creating this website: to help other students.

The person behind this project

I am a 17-year-old girl who has recently finished school and is looking forward to starting university. I love Biology, so I started thinking about how I could spend my free time doing something I enjoy. And if I can also help other people along the way, that makes me even happier.
